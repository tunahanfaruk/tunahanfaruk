# Xorviex

### Set the goal. Xorviex spawns the workforce.

<p>
<b>A self-architecting AI executive.</b><br />
You give it a mission in plain language. It designs the team that mission needs,<br />
sends them out to research and <i>act</i> with real tools, and lets nothing<br />
reach the real world until another AI has signed off on it.
</p>

<h2 align="center">Pricing</h2>

<div align="center">

Work is measured in <b>Operation Units (OU)</b> — one unit is one thing an agent did:<br />
a page scraped, a message sent, a draft written, a review performed.

<br />

<picture>
  <source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="assets/pricing-dark.svg" />
  <img src="assets/pricing-light.svg" alt="Starter: 15M OUs per month, 5 concurrent missions, standard models. Scale: 100M OUs, 25 concurrent missions, plus frontier models. Enterprise: custom on all three." width="880" />
</picture>

<sub>For scale: a real mission — one goal, four to six agents, live research plus a review on every output — runs about <b>1.3M OUs</b> end to end.</sub>
